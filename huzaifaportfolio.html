01 Summary

From concept to production floor

I'm a mechanical engineer with a degree from the Pakistan Institute of Engineering and Applied Sciences (PIEAS) and two years developing CNC-manufactured steel products at NUST's National Science & Technology Park. I work across the full product lifecycle — concept, mechanical design, material selection, fabrication planning, CNC coordination, assembly, testing, and deployment.

My focus is design that actually survives contact with the shop floor: understanding how a drawing becomes a weldment, where a tolerance stack-up will bite, and what changes when a part moves from prototype to production run. Internships at Fauji Fertilizer Company and a sugar processing plant in Sadiqabad added hands-on exposure to industrial machinery, preventive maintenance, and continuous production environments.
